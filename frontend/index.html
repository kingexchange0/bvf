Security

Security you can rely on

Your account and data are protected at every step.

Encrypted in transit

All traffic between your browser and BrightVale is encrypted in transit, and passwords are stored only as strong hashes — never in plain text.

Verified sessions

Every request is authenticated with a signed token that expires automatically, so only you can access your account.

Identity verification (KYC)

Identity documents are reviewed to help keep accounts genuine.

Monitored access

Administrative actions are logged and reviewed, and sensitive operations require authorization.
