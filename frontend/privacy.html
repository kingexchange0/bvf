Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Please note: investment returns follow plan terms and prevailing market conditions; yields may rise or fall with market performance. Review the Terms of Service for full details.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored only as a cryptographic hash).
  • Verification documents you upload for identity review (KYC), such as an ID document and proof of address.
  • Transaction records created through the platform: deposits, withdrawals, transfers, investments, and loan requests.
  • Support messages you exchange with our support team.

How we use it

  • To operate your account and provide the features you request.
  • To review identity documents and deposit confirmations as part of our approval workflows.
  • To respond to support requests and send account-related notifications.
  • To maintain activity logs used for security review, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not access your account without authorization, and administrative access is logged.

Data storage and security

Data is stored in secured databases operated for the platform. Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes, all traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to the API requires authenticated, expiring sessions. Uploaded verification documents are stored with restricted access and are visible only to you and authorized reviewers.

Your choices

You can update your profile information or request deletion of your account by contacting us at support@brightvale.finance. Your browser's local storage holds your login token and theme preference; clearing site data signs you out.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Continued use of the platform means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
